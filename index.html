Welcome to Our Green Home

The Earth is our common home. With the development of industrialization and urbanization, environmental pollution and ecological damage are becoming increasingly serious. Protecting the environment has become our urgent responsibility.

This website is dedicated to promoting environmental awareness, calling on everyone to start with small things in their daily lives to jointly protect our beautiful planet.

What Can We Do?

  • Conserve Water: Turn off the tap when not in use to reduce water waste.
  • Sort Waste: Separate recyclables, kitchen waste, hazardous waste, and other trash.
  • Green Commuting: Choose to walk, bike, or use public transportation.
  • Reduce Single-Use: Cut down on single-use plastics like bags, straws, and cutlery.
  • Save Energy: Turn off lights when leaving a room and use energy-efficient appliances.
